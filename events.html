West Coast event lead found: the Great War Historical Society (GWHS), based in Southern California, is the West Coast counterpart to the GWA — they run WWI trench warfare reenactments, educational events, and already count a German infantry unit ("3 Kompanie, IR 23") among their member units. Their two major annual events are in March (4th weekend) and November (1st weekend). This is the most promising regional lead so far — worth reaching out directly to inquire about attending, even as a spectator first. Site: gwhs1918.org.
Before attending any event: please read and sign the unit's Liability Waiver (Word document).
